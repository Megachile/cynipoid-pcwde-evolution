# Public supporting-data projection

The current paper uses 158 expression profiles. Historical source files retain the original 160-profile export. The final staging step, `scripts/deposit_inputs/public_scope.py`, removes the two excluded Biorhiza pallida adult libraries from every TSV, including their historical `EXPSAMPLE-019` and `EXPSAMPLE-020` rows. The retained roster carries the original public `sample_id` explicitly, preventing renumbering. The expression producer reads these stable assignments and checks the retained roster, metadata, crosswalk and observation counts. No TPM, retained observation, gene assignment or scientific cohort changes.

The projection removes only `mc_v1` and `cynogv4_id` fields, retaining CynOGv3 identifiers and all other cells. The Telenomus mapping checksum used by the CAZy producer is re-pinned in `config/cazy_scans.tsv`; the raw source and its original checksum stay in `config/cazy_stage_sources.tsv` for staging provenance. Three origin-domain FASTAs receive CynOGv3 aliases without changing any bytes. The origin-tree renderer reads those aliases.

`config/public_retired_inputs.txt` lists the 21 enumerated obsolete/historical files plus ten further unused historical inputs identified by the complete consumer audit: three old trimmed-domain comparisons, three expression inputs discarded before publication, three catalytic tables overwritten by current products, and one unused caption (Table S7); Table S1 caption remains consumed. The current reader expression and catalytic products keep their same-build producers; table rendering uses the existing authority directory as its fallback container, with author captions and built tables. These are omitted from new staging, never deleted from their originals or the reviewed baseline. The reader deposit copies the projected input layer; its source-panel, synteny, expression and other current tables continue to come from their committed current producers. Metadata and inventories retain their validation and packaging functions. `config/public_scope_pins.tsv` records the old/new file hashes and the method. `config/logical_inputs.tsv` is refreshed by the existing `stage.py --pin` route and bound by `data_root.py`.

`public_scope_policy.py` rejects retired files, old FASTA names, legacy columns and excluded samples. It runs at raw and projected reader-input validation. `test_public_scope.py` covers every transformation class, retained high-precision cells, unchanged FASTA bytes, excluded sample/identifier re-entry and missing source files. The closure gate rejects unread declared inputs and reader copies without a build read. No validation-only read is added to make a scientific payload appear consumed.

Supplement reader changes are confined to serialization: clearer column labels, B-lectin/PI-PLC-X names, tissue labels, one decimal for S6 percentiles read from the same-build producer-owned full-precision lineage intermediate (preventing double rounding) and two decimals for S7 gains per genome. Existing scientific TSVs keep their original values and schemas; additional precision intermediates preserve unrounded producer values. Table S8 has only the author caption; Table S7 uses the supplied final caption. The manuscript and supplement author exports are pinned at 20261118T0422Z and 20261118T0358Z, respectively; existing paragraph routes still fill numbers from the same scientific producers.
