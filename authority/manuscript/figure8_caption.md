**Figure 8.** **Tissue-specific expression of bacterially derived genes in Cynipini.** Tissue-specific expression in transcripts per million (TPM) of three laterally transferred PCWDEs (PL1_8, PL4_4, GH5_2), one ancestrally inherited carbohydrate digestive enzyme (GH1_e72), and bacterially-derived B-lectin in Cynipini species *Disholcaspis cinerosa* and *Belonocnema kinseyi*. Tissues are distinguished by life stage (larval vs. adult) and tissue type (gut, Malpighian tubule, salivary gland in larvae, venom gland, accessory gland, and carcass without glands in adults). PCWDE paralogs are distinguished by genomic locus in *Belonocnema kinseyi*. Asterisks indicate that *Disholcaspis cinerosa* transcripts were matched to each *Belonocnema* paralog by tree placement, not genomic synteny.
