# Model-less coordinate recovery (frozen layer 2)

Recover coordinates of frozen accepted copies lacking an ordered model, without defining blocks or changing membership. The downstream consumer is validate_modelless_frames.py, followed by produce_existing_recovery.py's post-definition scan. Searches are not part of make paper: the three recovery outputs are hashed heavy inputs in config/synteny_existing_sources.tsv. RECOVERY_PINS.tsv identifies retained query FASTAs, searched assemblies and outputs. Assembly hashes are the values verified by the producing jobs, not inferred from filenames.

The first driver enumerated all 675 accepted members against their own pinned source annotations, then attempted the nine explicitly missing coordinates with miniprot 0.13-r248, -t 4 -N 100 --outn=100 --outs=0 --outc=0.1. Rank by AS across the genome; require a unique best locus, identity >=0.95 and query coverage >=0.90. Job 1424511 exhausted memory while indexing DK; four completed alignments were retained. Its interrupted annotation audit and raw PL1_8 rescue extraction were finished by the continuation, not replaced by new membership inference.

Job 1424546 searched only the five unfinished queries with BLAST+ 2.16.0 tBLASTn subject mode, -seg no -evalue 1e-5 -num_threads 4 -max_target_seqs 1000000; subject mode ignored threads. Split each assembly into 2 Mb windows with 200 kb overlap, first window as a measured pilot, subsequent serial batches <=10 Mb. Deduplicate HSPs by original scaffold and query/subject endpoints; globally rank collinear HSP chains by summed bit score, maximum genomic gap 100 kb, and unique best locus. The original strict chain disallowed overlapping query HSPs; retain its per-copy status as sensitivity.

rechain_overlap_tolerant.py reuses all retained HSPs for all five queries, with <=30 aa query / <=90 bp genomic overlap. Coverage is union query-residue coverage; identity in this driver is summed identities / summed alignment length. Job 1424668 independently checked conservative unique-residue identity bounds and global rankings; both overlap-sensitive queries still exceed 95% identity / 90% coverage. Three strict resolutions are unchanged; N. valhalla and Q. scolopiae become resolved (strict sensitivity unresolved). No assembly search was repeated. Exact code, ties and all competing chains are retained in the producer run.

validate_modelless_frames.py consumes COMPLETE_COORDINATE_RECOVERY.tsv, RAW_LOCUS_RESCUE.tsv and OVERLAP_TOLERANT_RECHAIN.tsv. It checks native coordinates against the frozen order, lifts two pre-RagTag intervals with AGP plus exact sequence validation, and locates the AQP interval by a unique exact sequence match. Absent order scaffolds remain explicitly unscannable. Only accepted no-order copies enter MODELLESS_LOCI_DISPOSITION.tsv; the raw PL1_8 rescue remains separate. The production scan requires the entire locus inside the detected region's gene-span bounds; member cells are already PRESENT and have no recorded search-region interval.

Input pins. validate_modelless_frames.py and rechain_overlap_tolerant.py check every file they read against RECOVERY_PINS.tsv before derivation, through recovery_pins.py: the frozen gene order, the three recovery tables, the native, AGP and RagTag inputs for A. erinacei (GCA_035041075.1), Druon (GCA_010014905.2) and AQP (GCA_035222095.1), each FASTA index the frame checks open, and the five retained tBLASTn HSP tables and queries. Presence of every required pin is checked before any hash; a missing or changed pin stops the run before any output is written, and any unverified read fails. Pins are the historical values: repository and reference-genome records where they exist, otherwise the provenance record of the run that produced the retained outputs. The native Druon assembly (genomes/druon/druon_genome.fna) has no full-SHA256 record; its pin is that run's provenance fingerprint (size plus first and last 16 MiB), and its lifted interval is also checked for exact sequence identity against the fully pinned Druon RagTag assembly. The search drivers are retained unchanged as historical code and are not re-run.
